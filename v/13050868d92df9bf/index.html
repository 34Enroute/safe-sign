Safe Signer

Signs one Safe transaction (SafeTx, EIP-712) with your browser wallet, offline. Use it with tools/safe-exec.sh: propose makes the .env file, this page makes the .sig file, and exec submits it.

Opened from:
This must be a git clone you control, or the exact hosted address (with its sha256 in the path) that your own records list. Never a copy of unknown origin.

3. Compare the hash

safeTxHash, computed in this page from the fields above:

It must equal the hash safe-exec.sh propose printed. After you connect, the page also asks the Safe itself (getTransactionHash, through your wallet's RPC).

4. Connect and sign

Before you approve in the wallet: the wallet's own screen is what you are signing, not this page. Compare it, field by field, with the transaction as the proposing machine shows it (its /env page or terminal), not with this page: primaryType SafeTx; chainId; verifyingContract (the Safe); to; value; data; operation 0; nonce; and safeTxGas, baseGas, gasPrice all 0, gasToken, refundReceiver both the zero address. Expand every address in full.

On a phone: copy it, then open the /return address the executing machine printed and paste it there.

5. Execute (optional)

When enough owners have signed, any funded account can submit the transaction: the signatures carry the authority, the connected account only pays gas. Paste the signatures (one per line) or load the .sig files. Your own signature from step 4 is added automatically.
